Straight answers

What else would you want to know?

Which languages does Diya cover?

Spanish is our established starting language. Our phone tryout also includes Mandarin, Vietnamese, Arabic, Filipino, French, Hindi, Korean, Portuguese and Russian. For customer deployments, we confirm language quality and readiness for the conversations your team needs to support. Your existing human interpreter service remains available for other languages.

Do staff and patients speak naturally?

Yes. Diya provides real-time, two-way spoken interpretation. People talk and hear the interpretation aloud.

Do staff need to install anything or change their clinical workflow?

No. Staff use their existing phones to call Diya instead of their current interpreter number. No app, software installation or IT integration is needed. Optional browser or QR access is also available.

What happens when we need a human interpreter?

Your existing human interpreter service stays in place. During setup, we configure the connection to your human provider. Once configured, staff can press 0 during a Diya phone call to reach that service. With optional browser or QR access, they can select Human on screen.

How does the $0.20 pricing work?

Diya AI interpretation costs $0.20 per minute. You continue paying your human interpreter provider under your existing arrangement for the calls they handle. We'll confirm billing details and any additional terms before you begin service.

Does Diya work on speakerphones?

Yes. Staff can use a familiar phone on speaker for an in-person conversation. You should hear the experience using the actual devices and conditions in which your team will use it.

How are privacy and clinical oversight addressed?

Diya is built around healthcare-specific safeguards, including Microsoft Azure infrastructure, transient audio handling, data-purge controls, language-specific quality review and human escalation. We provide security and governance documentation for your organization's review.

Are there terms or an evaluation period?

You can request a short phone tryout to experience Diya firsthand. For organizational use, we'll confirm the evaluation scope, service terms and any commercial arrangements together.
