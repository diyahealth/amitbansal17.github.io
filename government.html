Government Language Access

AI and professional interpreter access for government communication

Diya brings AI interpretation, professional interpreter access, and governed multilingual communication into one language-access experience.

Healthcare taught us the hard parts of language access.

Healthcare has been Diya's proving ground for language access in consequential environments.

Translating words is only part of the job. Language access also has to fit the workflow, protect sensitive information, bring in professional interpreters when needed, and give the organization a way to manage quality, risk, and accountability.

Government operations are different. Many of those surrounding language-access problems are already familiar.

That means a government deployment does not have to start from zero.

As language AI advances, Diya is built to carry those gains forward.

The world's largest technology companies continue to invest at enormous scale in speech, translation, reasoning, and related AI capabilities.

Diya is built to harness that progress rather than ask each organization to recreate it for itself.

The AI provides the capability. Diya makes it fit the job.

The underlying AI can provide increasingly powerful speech, translation, and reasoning. Diya turns those capabilities into a usable language-access service — how an interaction begins, which language and service path is used, when a professional interpreter joins, how the workflow continues, and how quality and operations are governed.

As the underlying AI improves, those gains can flow through Diya to the people using it — through qualification and governance — without rebuilding the surrounding language-access service around every new model or provider.

Ask for language access. AI and professional interpreters are part of the same experience.

The user should not have to decide in advance whether the answer is AI or a professional interpreter. The need is understanding.

Sometimes AI is appropriate. Sometimes a professional interpreter is the right choice from the start. Sometimes the need changes during the conversation.

Diya is designed for all three.

AI and professional interpretation are part of the same language-access experience, so moving between them is part of the service rather than a separate system bolted on afterward.

Start with an established foundation. Engineer and validate what the agency requires.

Diya's production platform today is connected. A federal deployment may add requirements such as offline operation, specific device configurations, federal hosting, security controls, and authorization. Those requirements are engineered and validated for the selected operating environment.

  • Language + service orchestration

Defined and validated for the agency

  • Managed-device + identity configuration

  • Field testing + acceptance criteria

Language access goes where your people go.

Government work happens in offices, vehicles, field locations, public-facing settings, and places that were never designed around language access.

The service has to travel with the people doing the work.

For agencies evaluating Mobile Language Translation Services (MLTS), Diya provides the operating foundation for connected AI language access, professional interpreter pathways, managed-device deployment, language-quality governance, and operational visibility.

Device, connectivity, offline, security, and field-performance requirements are then engineered and validated for the agency's operating environment.

Federal deployment is defined around the agency environment.

Every agency has its own technology, security, device, data, and authorization requirements.

Diya works within that environment with the agency and its delivery partners — providing the language-access platform, technical evidence, integration support, and product expertise needed for the deployment.

Start with the language need. Validate it where the work happens. Expand from evidence.
